💸 Refund Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy covers ProTools Pro (₹149/month), paid through our Instamojo hosted checkout. One payment buys one month (30 days) of Pro. There is no auto-renewal — you pay again each month only if you want to continue.

1. When you get a refund

  • Double payment (paid twice by mistake): report it within 7 days and the duplicate payment will be refunded.
  • Paid, but Pro not active: check your order status first (keep your payment ID). If the gateway shows PAID and Pro is still not active, report it via the Contact page — we verify the record and activate Pro.

2. When there is no refund

Pro is a digital subscription. Once the paid month has started, there is no refund — except for a double payment reported within 7 days (see above).

3. Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. Pro has no auto-renewal: simply do not pay next month and Pro ends on its own. The free tools stay free forever.

4. How to ask for a refund

Write to us via the Contact page with the email you used at checkout and your payment ID. We reply after verifying the payment record.
