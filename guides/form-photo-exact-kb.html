Form photo ko exact KB me kaise karein

Form photo ka size galat hone par form aage hi nahi badhta. Is guide me samjho ki KB size ka matlab kya hai, target kaise chunein, aur kin galtiyon se bachein.

⚠️ Har form ke notification me size alag ho sakta hai — apne form ke notification me diya hua exact size check karein, phir wahi target chunein. Kisi guide ya purane form ka size apne form par lagu mat karo.

File size (KB) aur photo ka size (pixels) — farq samjho

KB batata hai ki file kitni bhari hai — kitna data store hoga. Pixels batate hain ki photo kitni chaudi aur unchi hai. Ek hi photo ke do version ho sakte hain: ek 200 KB ka aur ek 35 KB ka, dono ke pixels ek jaise, bas quality (compression) alag. Form aksar dono cheezein mangta hai: ek range me KB, aur ek range me pixels. Isliye sirf KB dekhna kaafi nahi — notification me dono check karo.

Target size kaise chunein

  1. Apne form ka notification kholo aur photo ke liye di hui range padho — jaise 20 se 50 KB ke beech.
  2. Tool me range ke beech ka target chuno, na ki bilkul kinare ka. Beech ka target chhoti galtiyon se bacha leta hai — jaise 20–50 KB ki range me 35 KB ek surakshit target hai.
  3. Photo banane ke baad uska final size dobara check karo. Tool jo size bataye, wahi size upload screen par bhi dikhna chahiye.

Quality ka tradeoff — imaandari se

File chhoti karne ka matlab hai kuch detail hatana. Thoda kam karne par photo saaf hi dikhegi. Lekin bahut chhoti file banane par chehra dhundhla, text toota hua, ya photo pixelated dikhegi — aur aisi photo form par reject ho sakti hai, bhale hi KB range sahi ho. Isliye hamesha final photo ko zoom karke dekho: chehra saaf dikhna chahiye, aur photo fatni nahi chahiye.

Aam galtiyan jo form atakati hain

  • WhatsApp se bheji hui photo: WhatsApp photo ko pehle hi compress kar deta hai. Original photo se kaam karo, WhatsApp wali copy se nahi.
  • Screenshot: Screen ka screenshot lene se photo ki quality aur pixels dono bigadte hain. Seedhi photo file use karo.
  • Galat format: Form aksar JPG mangta hai. PNG photo ko JPG me badle bina upload mat karo — tool me JPG output chuno.
  • Purana size yaad rakhna: Pichhli baar ka size is baar bhi sahi hoga, ye zaroori nahi. Har form ka notification alag se check karo.
  • Live photo wale form par file upload: Kuch forms me photo camera se live leni hoti hai, file upload hoti hi nahi. Agar form live photo mang raha hai, to file resize karne se kaam nahi chalega — form ke steps dhyan se padho.

Step by step

  1. Notification se exact KB range note karo.
  2. Original JPG ya PNG photo chuno — WhatsApp copy ya screenshot nahi.
  3. Range ke beech ka target set karo.
  4. Final photo ko zoom karke quality check karo, phir download karke upload karo.

FAQs

Q: Photo upload hoti hai?
A: Nahi. Is site ke tools me photo aapke browser me hi process hoti hai, kisi server par upload nahi hoti.

Q: KB range se thoda upar-niche chalega?
A: Nahi — form ka system range ke bahar ki file ko rok deta hai. Range ke andar hi rakho, behtar hai beech me.

Q: Signature ke liye bhi yehi guide chalegi?
A: Tareeka wahi hai, lekin signature ki range alag hoti hai. Signature ke liye apne form ke notification me di hui range hi use karo.
