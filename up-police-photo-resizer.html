  • Size: 50KB se kam (ye tool 45-52KB target karta hai)

  • Background: Halka/safed background behtar hai

Limitations

  • Ye tool sirf size compress karta hai — background change ya face retouch nahi karta
  • Bahut chhoti photo (pehle se 50KB se kam) ko ye bada nahi karega
  • PNG photo ko JPG me convert kar dega
  • Tumhari photo browser me hi process hoti hai — server pe upload nahi hoti, 100% private

Common errors aur solutions

❌ "Photo select nahi ho rahi": Sirf JPG, PNG, ya WebP format use karo. HEIC (iPhone) format support nahi hai — pehle JPG me convert karo.

❌ "Size 50KB se zyada aa raha": Bahut detailed photo (jaise ped-paudhe wala background) me size zyada aa sakta hai. Simple background wali photo use karo.

❌ "Download nahi ho raha": Browser ka pop-up blocker off karo, ya doosra browser try karo.
