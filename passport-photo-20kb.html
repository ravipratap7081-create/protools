📋 Passport Photo 20KB — Complete Guide

Kahan-kaam ayega?

  • Sarkari forms: UP Scholarship, admit cards, online applications
  • Passport/visa: Online form ke liye chhoti photo
  • Exam forms: UP Board, SSC, Banking exams
  • Size: 17-22KB target (20KB standard)

Step-by-step kaise karein?

  1. Photo chuno: "Photo Select Karo" dabao — JPG ya PNG
  2. Auto compress: Tool 10-step me quality adjust karke ~20KB layega
  3. Check karo: Preview me photo dekho, size confirm karo
  4. Download: "Download 20KB Photo" button dabao

Practical example

Tumhari phone ki photo 3MB ki hai. Tool ise 600px tak chhota karega, phir quality ghata ke ~19KB ka JPG banayega. Face saaf dikhega, form reject nahi hoga.

Limitations

  • Sirf size kam karta hai — passport-size dimensions (3.5x4.5cm) crop nahi karta
  • Photo browser me process hoti hai — upload nahi hoti
  • 20KB bahut chhota hai — print ke liye use mat karo, sirf online form ke liye
  • Passport Seva (passport.gov.in) par photo 20–100KB hoti hai, max 300KB — ye tool 20KB exam/admit-card standard ke liye hai, Passport Seva ke liye alag size chahiye ho sakta hai. Checked: 10 Nov 2026.

Common errors

❌ "Photo blurry lag rahi": 20KB me thodi quality kam hogi hi — ye normal hai. Form ke liye chalegi.

❌ "iPhone photo nahi khul rahi": HEIC format ko pehle JPG me convert karo (phone settings me jaake).

FAQs

Q: 20KB photo print kar sakte hain?
A: Nahi, ye sirf online upload ke liye hai. Print ke liye original photo use karo.

Q: Kya dimensions bhi passport-size honge?
A: Nahi, ye sirf file size kam karta hai. Dimensions ke liye alag crop tool use karo.
