About ProTools

Free online tools that respect your privacy.

ProTools is a collection of free online utilities — PDF merge & split, photo resizers, resume maker, scheme finder and more. No signup, no watermark, no file uploads. Everything runs in your browser.

Our mission: Useful digital tools should be free, private and simple. Your files never leave your device.

Who these tools are for: students and job applicants preparing form uploads, small business owners sending bills and documents, and anyone who needs a quick PDF or photo fix without installing software or creating an account. The tools are deliberately simple: one job per page, no dashboards, no subscriptions.

How the tools work: every tool on this site runs in your browser using JavaScript. When you select a file, it is processed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, nothing is stored in an account, and closing the tab ends the session. That is why the tools work without signup — and also why very large files depend on your device's memory.

What the tools do not do: they do not edit text inside PDFs, do not turn scanned images into searchable text, do not remove passwords, and do not store your files. Photo tools adjust file size; they do not guarantee that a form will accept a photo, because every form's official notification sets its own size and dimension rules — always check that notification first.

How the site is kept free: the tools themselves are free with no paywall for the core job. If you find a bug or a tool that does not work, tell us through the Contact page — bug reports with the browser name and the steps you took help us fix things fastest.

Read our Guides for step-by-step help with merging, splitting, shrinking PDFs, and resizing form photos.
