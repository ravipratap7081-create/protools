❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF tool really free?

Yes — merging and splitting are 100% free with no signup and no watermark. Free file-size limits apply: 10 MB per PDF (Pro allows 25 MB).

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

As many as you like — just keep the total size reasonable for your device's memory (under ~100 MB works smoothly).

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked first — remove the password, then use this tool.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — the tool is fully responsive and works on Android, iPhone and desktop browsers.
